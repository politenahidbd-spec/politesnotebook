Most of my work, I now realise, is subtraction.

I take too many photographs, and then I throw most of them away. I write too many sentences, and then I cross most of them out. The days I like best are the ones I've slowly removed things from — meetings, opinions, plans — until what remains is small enough to see.

There is no method to this. Only the willingness to keep looking at what you've made and asking, quietly, whether it needs to be there.
